If you self-host Ringwise, you can now use Telnyx for phone numbers, calls and texts. Set `TELEPHONY_NUMBERS` to pick the countries you buy from Telnyx. You can buy US numbers from Telnyx and keep the rest on Twilio, or move every country.

Ringwise buys each Telnyx number and connects it to GPT-Live over encrypted SIP. Your receptionist answers calls, transfers callers to your team, and forwards the calls it can't take. Texts go through the same number, and Ringwise records what each call and text costs.

Hosted Ringwise stays on Twilio, so hosted customers don't need to change anything. To set up Telnyx, follow the [self-hosting guide](https://docs.ringwise.example/self-hosting/providers).
