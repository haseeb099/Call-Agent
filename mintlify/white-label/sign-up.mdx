You sign up for White Label on Ringwise's own domain. Signup creates your Ringwise account if you don't have one, then your partner account on the Free tier. It reserves your dashboard address and opens the partner console.

<Note>
  Whoever runs a Ringwise deployment can limit partner signup to invitation links. Without one, the signup page says **Partner signup is invite-only for now. Ask Ringwise for a link.**
</Note>

## Create your partner account

Signup takes two short forms: your account, then your company.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the signup page">
    Go to `https://app.ringwise.example/partner/signup`, or open the invitation link Ringwise sent you. An invitation works only for the email address it names, after you verify that address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Enter your **Email** and a **Password**, then click **Create account**, or click **Continue with Google**. Ringwise emails you a code to verify your address. If you already have a Ringwise account, click **Sign in** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your company name">
    Under **Company name**, enter the name your clients know you by. It becomes your brand's name, which you can change later.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your dashboard address">
    Under **Dashboard address**, choose the subdomain your clients sign in at, such as `acme` for `acme.partners.ringwise.example`. Use 3 to 40 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the account">
    Click **Create partner account**. The console opens on the **Businesses** page.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't change your dashboard address in the console after signup, so pick one you'll keep. Names that belong to Ringwise, such as `www` or `app`, aren't available.

## Sign in to the console

Your team reaches the same console from two addresses:

- `https://app.ringwise.example/partner`, which shows Ringwise's brand. White Label's sign-in page is `https://app.ringwise.example/partner/login`.
- `/partner` on your dashboard address, such as `https://acme.partners.ringwise.example/partner`, which shows yours and signs you in on your brand's sign-in page.

Each address keeps its own sign-in, so you sign in once on each with the same email and password. Opening a business from Ringwise's domain takes you to your address, where you sign in again.

## Work for more than one partner

One Ringwise account can belong to several partners, and to businesses of its own. Click your partner's name at the top of the sidebar to switch partners, or choose **Sign up another partner** to create one more. On a partner's own address, the console opens only that partner.

## What happens next

A new partner starts on Free, whose pool and trial number the [tier table](/white-label/tiers#compare-the-tiers) lists. Before you create a business, [add your brand](/white-label/branding). To bill clients through Ringwise, [connect payments and create a package](/white-label/packages). When you need more minutes or phone numbers, [choose a paid tier](/white-label/tiers).
